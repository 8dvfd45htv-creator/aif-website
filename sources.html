How AI was used in this project

My teacher has said using AI is fine as long as I credit it, so here is exactly what it did.

  • This website. The code, layout and design of this site were built with Claude Code, an AI tool made by Anthropic. It arranged and condensed my own writing from my portfolio into web pages, built the charts from my survey numbers, and added the sources page. The research, data, diary entries, interviews and the findings are mine.
  • The interview transcript. I recorded my philosophy teacher's interview on my phone. A transcript was tidied with an AI tool for readability, and the quotes on this site come from that tidied version.
  • Feedback. I asked Claude to review my portfolio on time management. I describe what it said and what I did with it on the process page.
  • Editing and planning. Claude helped me plan, outline and edit parts of my portfolio and my Progress Check presentation. I wrote the analysis in my own words.

Anyone reading this should treat the research as something to check, not something to trust because it is on a school project. The links above go to the original sources.
